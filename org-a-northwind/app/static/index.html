Northwind sends an agent to do a job for Harbor Ops

Two separate companies. No shared login systems, no private network between them, no secrets in common. The only thing that crosses the boundary is a signed badge and a request.

Northwind Platform Google Cloud, Europe Harbor Ops AWS, Europe

Think of it like a work ID badge. Northwind issues the badge: it says who the holder is, which doors it may open, and who issued it. Harbor Ops, a completely different company on different computers, can check any badge at its own front desk, on the spot, without ever phoning Northwind. Cancelling a badge works the same way: Northwind cancels it centrally, and the next time Harbor checks, that badge no longer opens any door. (The technical name for this kind of badge is VAID; we will not use that word again on this page.)
